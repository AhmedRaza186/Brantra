# Brantra Product Scope

## Target user

Self-managed UGC creators and micro-influencers handling approximately 3–15 active brand deals without an agency.

## Core problem

Brand inquiries, deal terms, deadlines, revisions, usage rights, approvals, invoices, and payment follow-ups are scattered across DMs, emails, notes, calendars, and spreadsheets.

## Core workflow

Brand conversation → structured deal → deliverables → revisions and approval → invoice → payment → campaign history.

## MVP features

- Manual deal creation
- Paste a brand conversation
- Extract structured deal terms
- Creator confirmation before saving
- Deal pipeline
- Deliverables and deadlines
- Revision limits
- Usage-rights tracking
- Invoice and payment status
- Follow-up reminders
- Campaign history

## Non-goals

- Creator marketplace
- Influencer discovery
- Social-media scheduling
- Analytics platform
- Agency management
- Generic CRM
- Automatic negotiation
- Full accounting software
